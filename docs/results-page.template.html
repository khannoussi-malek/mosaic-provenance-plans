Cheap first, escalate on failure

Which works in the National Gallery of Art passed between 1933 and 1945 through a person or firm on the 1946 ALIU Red Flag Names List, and how should a system spend compute to answer that reliably?

Seven ways of answering were run on 100 provenance texts with local models on one laptop, scored against the museum's own owner list, and compared with 95% intervals. Every answer quotes the sentence it rests on, for an expert to judge.

Not affiliated with HPI or MIT. Built with an AI coding assistant; see “How this was made” below.

Limits

  • Small and single-machine: 100 + 20 documents, one laptop, two open models; screening intervals are wide, and 15 of the 22 flags involve one dealer.
  • No domain expert: screening labels come from two Claude agents of one model family, which share errors; owner scores rest on the museum's own data, which is incomplete.
  • Owner matching in the scorer is approximate (it links foundations to their founders); its error is unmeasured and may be the size of the cascade's gain.
  • Three scoring definitions were corrected after seeing results, for all plans alike; before and after are kept.
  • Output is candidates and evidence for experts, never a finding about a person or a restitution claim.

How this was made

I built the study with an AI coding assistant (Claude Code). I set the goals and constraints (public data, local models at zero cost, measured comparisons), made the decisions and reviewed the results; the assistant wrote most of the code, ran the experiments and drafted the analyses. The plans ran on local models; Claude also made the reference labels, judged the Chicago answers and read the texts once for the graph. Every number on this page can be recomputed from the repository.

Rerun it

python3 -u cascade.py qwen3:8b qwen2.5:14b data/testset_nga.json out/run_P3.json
python3 results.py          # every number on this page, with intervals
python3 ask.py "Which paintings passed through a Red Flag dealer between 1933 and 1945?"

All code, data and cached model answers are in the project folder (demo/README.md). With the cache, every number recomputes in seconds; without it, in about two hours on an Apple M4 Pro.
